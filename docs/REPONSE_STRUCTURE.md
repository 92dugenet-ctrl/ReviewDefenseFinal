# ReviewDefense — Réponse

## Purpose

`pages/reponse.html` explains how ReviewDefense turns an analyzed review into a response that can be personalized and validated by the professional.

Narrative:

1. Répondre, sans répondre automatiquement.
2. Comprendre avant d'écrire.
3. Quel type de réponse ?
4. La réponse se construit.
5. Votre ton. Votre marque.
6. Vous gardez les mots.
7. Une réponse. Plusieurs façons de la dire.
8. La réponse reste sous votre contrôle.
9. Répondre ou agir.
10. Des réponses qui ressemblent à votre entreprise.

## Visual principles

- The same review remains the narrative anchor.
- The response appears progressively instead of as a generic AI chat.
- The product interface is introduced only after the reasoning is established.
- White/off-white background, deep navy type, restrained blue-compatible accents.
- Fine borders, generous whitespace, minimal UI chrome.
- Motion explains the transition from understanding to writing.
- No generic SaaS card wall and no oversized chatbot.

## Product principles

- A negative review does not automatically produce a response.
- The response follows understanding of intent, tone and context.
- The AI recommends a direction and proposes copy.
- The professional can edit the response.
- Validation remains human-controlled before publication.
- If the situation calls for another action, the user can move toward the appropriate flow instead of responding automatically.
